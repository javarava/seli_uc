Seli Market Privacy Policy

Seli Trading Company

Effective Date: August 26, 2026  |  Last Updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Seli Trading Company ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use the Seli Market mobile application, web services at seli.com.ng, and associated platforms (collectively, the "Service").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using Seli Market, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms, please do not access or use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information depending on whether you interact with the Service as a Buyer, a Merchant, or a Content Creator:

A. Personal Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, phone number, username, and account credentials upon registration.
  • Merchant Verification: Business name, address, government-issued IDs (e.g., NIN, BVN, or registration details where required) for verified merchant status.
  • Transaction Data: Bank account numbers, payment credentials (processed securely via regulated payment gateways), billing addresses, and escrow transaction logs.
  • User Media Content: Product photographs, short videos, video spotlights, product copy, and buyer-seller chat communications.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Technical Data: Device model, OS version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and system diagnostic logs.
  • Usage Analytics: In-app interaction metrics, items viewed, video watch duration, and search history inside Seli Market.
  • Location Data: Approximate location (derived from IP) or precise geolocation (with explicit consent) to connect you with nearby merchants and deliver accurate logistics quotes.

C. Information Processed by Shemi AI

Media, raw photos, audio, or text uploaded by merchants to Shemi AI are processed to automatically generate product copy, search tags, video scripts, and marketing collateral.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data to maintain, optimize, and secure Seli Market, including:

  • Facilitating registration, purchases, payments, inventory tracking, and escrow settlements.
  • Powering Shemi AI features to build automated product catalogs and marketing collateral.
  • Personalizing search recommendations, video feeds, and localized deal discovery.
  • Preventing fraud, verifying seller authenticity, and maintaining platform security.
  • Managing user rewards under the "Shop-to-Earn" and "Create-to-Earn" mechanics.
  • Sending essential order alerts, transaction confirmations, and security notices.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We disclose data strictly under the following operational guidelines:

  • Buyer-Merchant Order Fulfillment: Shipping information (name, phone, delivery address) is shared with merchants and logistics partners to execute fulfillment.
  • Third-Party Infrastructure: Secure processors including regulated payment gateways, Cloudflare R2 media hosting, and Supabase database services.
  • Legal Obligations: Compliance with applicable court orders, regulatory disclosures, or statutory enforcement requests.

4. Security and Retention

We deploy end-to-end transport layer encryption (SSL/TLS), strict database firewalls, and role-based access controls to safeguard user information. Personal data is retained for the duration of active account status or as mandated by financial accounting regulations.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data. You can delete your account or manage mobile device permissions (camera, location, media) at any time through the in-app account settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Seli Market is strictly intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from minors.

7. Updates to This Policy

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy at any time. Updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp, along with in-app notifications for material updates.
